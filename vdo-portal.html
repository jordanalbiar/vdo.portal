A QUICK OVERVIEW BEFORE YOU JOIN
📡 Direct Peer-to-Peer Connection
VDO.Ninja connects your device directly to the people you call, like a phone call, not a video platform. This means participants may see your IP address.
🎥 We Don't Record Your Sessions
Your video, audio, and chat are not stored by VDO.Ninja or the VDO Portal.
🔑 Your Room Name Is Your Key
Treat your room name like a password. Anyone with it can join. Add a password for extra security, and avoid sharing links publicly.
Full details: docs.vdo.ninja/privacy
